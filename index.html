I am an undergraduate in philosophy at National Taiwan University, expecting my B.A. in June 2027. My current interests are:

  • Metaphysics (esp. social ontology, nonexistence and object theory, modality, and higher-order metaphysics)
  • Metametaphysics (esp. metaontology, essence, grounding, and generalized identity)
  • Philosophical logic (esp. higher-order languages and logic, granularity, and truthmaker semantics)
  • Philosophy of art (esp. ontology of repeatable artworks, performance, fiction, and interpretive and critical practices)

My current projects concern essence and necessity, truthmaker semantics and the logic of belief revision, and the nature of metaphysical anchoring.
